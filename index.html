Lionel Messi

A tribute to the boy from Rosario who became a football legend

Biography

Lionel Andrés Messi was born on 24 June 1987 in Rosario, Argentina. He started playing football as a small child and joined the youth team of Newell's Old Boys. At a young age he was diagnosed with a growth hormone problem, and FC Barcelona offered to pay for his treatment. At 13 years old he moved to Spain to join Barcelona's youth academy, La Masia.

He made his first-team debut for Barcelona in 2004 and went on to become the club's greatest player. In 2021 he joined Paris Saint-Germain, and in 2023 he moved to Inter Miami in the United States. For Argentina, he finally reached the top of world football by lifting the 2022 World Cup.

Achievements

  • 8 Ballon d'Or awards, the most by any player
  • 4 UEFA Champions League titles with Barcelona
  • 10 La Liga titles with Barcelona
  • Olympic gold medal with Argentina in 2008
  • Copa America winner in 2021 and 2024
  • FIFA World Cup winner in 2022, and Golden Ball winner of the tournament

Timeline

  1. 1987 Born in Rosario, Argentina
  2. 2000 Moves to Spain and joins Barcelona's La Masia academy
  3. 2004 Makes his debut for the Barcelona first team
  4. 2008 Wins Olympic gold with Argentina
  5. 2009 Wins his first Ballon d'Or
  6. 2021 Wins the Copa America and joins Paris Saint-Germain
  7. 2022 Wins the FIFA World Cup in Qatar
  8. 2023 Joins Inter Miami and wins his 8th Ballon d'Or
